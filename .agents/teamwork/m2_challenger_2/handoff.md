## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Adversarial Gating on Multi-Guest Stays**:
   - In Challenge 1 (`tests/m2-challenger2-adversarial.test.mjs`), a 2-guest reservation (Titular + Companion) arrived today.
   - When dates changed, both guests were marked `needsReSignature = true`.
   - `GET /api/reception/today` returned `canAuthorizeEntry: false`, `allCheckinDone: false`, `entryBadgeType: "error"`.
   - Attempting `POST /api/reception/checkin/9001` with empty body, `{ partial: true }`, or `{ guestIndices: [1] }` was rejected with HTTP 400 and `needsReSignature: true`.
   - When Guest 1 re-signed via `POST /api/pms/pre-checkin`, Guest 1 cleared, but Guest 2 remained pending. The server kept `r.needsReSignature = true`, preventing premature reception check-in.
   - Only after Guest 2 re-signed did `r.needsReSignature` switch to `false`, allowing `GET /api/reception/today` to yield `canAuthorizeEntry: true`, and `POST /api/reception/checkin/9001` to return HTTP 200 with `status: "in_house"`.

2. **07:00 Morning Routine & Operational Schedulers**:
   - In Challenge 2, Res 9002 was moved from 10/11–15/11 to 12/11–18/11 and flat transferred from 201 to 202.
   - `morningEmailSentDate`, `morningEmailSentAt`, `receptionNotifiedAt`, and `vehicle.garageNotifiedAt` were deleted. Simulated evaluation on the new checkin date (12/11) confirmed the reservation qualifies for the 07:00 arrival trigger.
   - Breakfast on 10/11 was automatically cancelled with reason `"Check-in alterado do dia 10/11 para 12/11..."`. Breakfast on 13/11 and 15/11 remained `pending` and moved to Flat 202. Breakfast on 17/11 (previously out-of-bounds) was re-activated to `pending` in Flat 202.
   - Housekeeping turnover request #950 was transferred to the new checkout date 18/11 and reassigned to Flat 202.

3. **Zero-Cost Guest Portal Modification**:
   - In Challenge 3, date shifting with identical night count on direct reservation #9003 immediately invalidated prior check-in (`needsReSignature: true`, `fnhrCompleted: false`) and wiped `morningEmailSentDate`.

4. **Twin Server Consistency**:
   - Both server copies maintain exact SHA-256 hash `8b6a5d9e37a2f89650e4613558dbaf1c51f795193d75d04e216bb41b3bbee128`, satisfying the zero-divergence requirement.
